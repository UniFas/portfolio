How I Design

Horror isn't just what the player sees. It's what the player believes might happen.

I approach horror as a system of uncertainty, vulnerability, and anticipation. Rather than relying on constant threats, I explore how mechanics, environmental cues, and player decisions can create tension, even when nothing is happening.

01 / CONTROL

Player Vulnerability

How much control should the player have? When should that control feel reliable, and when should it become uncertain?

02 / TENSION

Anticipation & Release

Building tension through pacing, uncertainty, and carefully designed moments where the player can finally breathe.

03 / PERCEPTION

Environmental Storytelling

Using spaces, visual cues, and atmosphere to encourage players to question what they see and what they think they understand.

The Designer

I'm Francis.
I make worlds feel alive.

I'm a game designer and developer currently studying Game Design in Leiria, Portugal. My work brings together gameplay systems, technical implementation, and atmospheric storytelling.

I'm especially interested in horror experiences where the mechanics themselves create emotion, where the way a player moves, explores, and makes decisions becomes part of the storytelling.

Alongside game design, I work across programming, 3D creation, and worldbuilding, allowing me to approach projects from both creative and technical perspectives.
