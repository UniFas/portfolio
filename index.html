Featured system

What if horror events didn't just happen… but asked permission?

Unreliable Reality is a reusable horror system built around tension, controlled unpredictability and environmental manipulation. Individual objects can request permission to perform unsettling actions, while a central Horror Director evaluates whether the current moment is right.

NEW · IN DEVELOPMENT
  1. 01 / REQUEST

    Objects ask

    An object detects an opportunity, such as a painting when the player leaves the room, and requests an event.

  2. 02 / EVALUATE

    The director judges

    It weighs tension, event history, cooldowns, randomness and context to decide if the moment is right.

  3. 03 / DECIDE

    Approve or deny

    The request is approved or quietly denied. The player never sees the decision, and that absence is part of the design.

How I design

Horror isn't just what the player sees. It's what the player believes might happen.

I approach horror as a system of uncertainty, vulnerability, and anticipation. Rather than relying on constant threats, I explore how mechanics, environmental cues, and player decisions can create tension, even when nothing is happening.

01 / CONTROL

Player Vulnerability

How much control should the player have? When should that control feel reliable, and when should it become uncertain?

02 / TENSION

Anticipation & Release

Building tension through pacing, uncertainty, and carefully designed moments where the player can finally breathe.

03 / PERCEPTION

Environmental Storytelling

Using spaces, visual cues, and atmosphere to encourage players to question what they see and what they think they understand.
