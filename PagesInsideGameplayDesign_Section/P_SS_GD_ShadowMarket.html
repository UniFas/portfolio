01 THE EXPERIENCE

A shop that sells
what people can’t name.

Each night, strangers find your lantern. They bring a request that sounds almost ordinary. Your shelves hold objects that might help, or make the ache worse.

There is no combat. No chase. Just a small stall, a handful of choices, and a world that remembers every transaction. The horror gathers slowly in the details: a light gone dim, a reflection that lingers, a familiar face returning with something changed.
