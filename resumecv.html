Career overview

Memorable player experiences, built to be tested.

I am a game designer driven by the craft of creating memorable player experiences. Over the past years, I've studied and explored multiple areas of game design: monetization models and gamification, data analysis, level design, combat systems, and production documentation.

My approach focuses on iteration, clarity, and delivering meaningful interactions that respect the player's journey. I bring structured problem-solving, strong communication, and a passion for turning ideas into playable, testable, and enjoyable systems.
