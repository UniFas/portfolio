Devlogs

One game I won't give up on.

Beneath the Questline is a horror game where the player accepts quests that are never what they seem. It is my own take on the kind of game that Bendy and the Ink Machine and Poppy Playtime are, and it is the project closest to my heart.

THE HOOK

Accept the quest. It is never what it seems.

The core idea is trust: the player says yes to a request, and the game spends the rest of its time making them regret how easily they did.

Where it stands

Paused, not dropped.

I have not posted or worked on it much lately. Coursework, reading for my internship and getting faster at closing work took the time. This is not a project I plan to give up on.

  2. NOW

  3. NEXT
