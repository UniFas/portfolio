Devlogs

One game I won't give up on.

Beneath the Questline is a horror game where the player accepts quests that are never what they seem. It is my own take on the kind of game that Bendy and the Ink Machine and Poppy Playtime are, and it is the project closest to my heart.

The project

A lot of design already exists, and it is detailed.

Before the first line of final code, I wanted the game on paper, properly.

91+

pages of game design document

The GDD for Beneath the Questline runs past 91 pages. It is the backbone I keep coming back to, and there is still a lot in it left to implement.

QUEST DESIGN

3 quests, written in great detail

Three of the game's quests are laid out in full on my narrative design showcase, so you can see how a quest that looks innocent turns on the player.

Open the quest showcase →
THE HOOK

Accept the quest. It is never what it seems.

The core idea is trust: the player says yes to a request, and the game spends the rest of its time making them regret how easily they did.

Where it stands

Paused, not dropped.

I have not posted or worked on it much lately. Coursework, reading for my internship and getting faster at closing work took the time. This is not a project I plan to give up on.

  1. DONE

    Full production and the first devlogs

    Beneath the Questline entered full production in 2025, and I started publishing developer logs on IndieDB.

  2. NOW

    On pause, and still improving

    The hurdles were real, but the time is not wasted. Everything I am building elsewhere feeds back into this game (see below).

  3. NEXT

    Back on the horse

    As soon as I can, I return to the GDD and keep implementing it, with new devlogs along the way.

What I am bringing back

What I learn elsewhere, this game gets.

Even while I am away from it, I am sharpening the skills it needs.

PLANNED

The Horror Director goes in

I plan to use my Horror Director from Unreliable Reality inside Beneath the Questline, so objects ask permission to scare the player and the game decides when the moment is right.

PLANNED

Implement the rest of the GDD

Much of the 91+ pages is still waiting to be built. The plan is to work through it system by system and show the progress in the devlogs.

MEANWHILE

Shipping and learning elsewhere

Once Upon a Page shipped on Steam, I spent time on Erasmus in Finland, and I keep growing as a designer and programmer, so I come back stronger.
